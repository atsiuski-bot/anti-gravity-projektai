# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Gildija serves blue-collar and field workers who primarily use phones, often outdoors, with gloves, older devices, and unreliable connectivity. Their core loop is to see assigned work, start or switch a session, and stop or finish it with confidence that every worked minute is preserved. Managers and administrators use wider screens for oversight, approvals, corrections, reporting, and team administration.

## Product Purpose

Gildija is a mobile-first work-time tracking tool for tasks, quick work, calls, breaks, shift planning, and team oversight. Its primary success condition is trustworthy time credit: the worker can always understand what is running, whether an action was accepted, how much time will be credited, and how the system recovers after offline use, reloads, device suspension, retries, or concurrent-device activity.

## Brand Personality

Calm, dependable, and direct. The interface should feel like a trustworthy field instrument: quiet during routine work, unmistakable when a session is active, and explicit whenever data is pending, conflicted, recovered, or rejected.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a decorative SaaS dashboard, a gamified productivity toy, or an interface that hides state behind subtle color and animation. Avoid tiny controls, dense mobile tables, ornamental gradients, glass surfaces, card-grid repetition, unfamiliar standard controls, raw technical errors, and optimistic timer states that look confirmed before durable credit is guaranteed.

## Design Principles

1. Trust before speed: immediate feedback must never misrepresent durable or server-confirmed state.
2. Calm canvas, loud state: routine chrome stays quiet while an active session remains unmistakable through color, text, and icon.
3. Recovery is part of the primary flow: offline, reload, suspension, retry, and multi-device conflict states must be understandable and recoverable by the worker.
4. One authority for credited time: every projection and total must reconcile to the canonical work-session ledger without double credit or silent loss.
5. Field usability is the baseline: prioritize sunlight legibility, glove-friendly targets, low-end phone performance, and formal plain Lithuanian copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA is mandatory. User-readable text is at least 12 px, primary information at least 14 px, touch targets are at least 44 by 44 px, focus is visible, normal-text contrast is at least 4.5:1, and color is never the only state signal. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences. Critical status and error messages must remain understandable to workers with limited technical vocabulary and under poor field conditions.
